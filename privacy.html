Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 18, 2026

InkPass is an app that helps people find tattoo shops that fit their style. It is made by Oswald Technologies LLC ("we", "us"), a Missouri company. This policy explains what information the InkPass app and this website collect, how we use it, and the choices you have.

The short version: we collect only what the app needs to work. We don't sell your information, we don't show ads, and we don't use advertising trackers.

Information you give us

  • Account details. Your email address and password when you sign up with email (passwords are stored only in hashed form by our sign-in provider), or your name, email address and profile photo from Google when you sign in with Google.
  • Profile. Optional first and last name, an optional profile photo you upload, and an optional home location (a city or ZIP code — never your exact address or GPS position).
  • Favorites. The shops you save, so they follow you across devices.
  • Event RSVPs and notification settings, if you use Nearby Events.
  • Shop accounts. If you claim a tattoo shop: the shop you claimed, the verification code check, and the details you add to the shop's card (phone, address, website, styles, walk-ins, photo, opening hours and events). Shop card details are shown publicly to other app users.
  • Messages you send us by email.

Information collected when you use the app

  • Quiz answers. When you finish Match My Style, we save your answers together with the ZIP code area you searched from and the shops we showed. These records are not linked to your account and contain no name or email. We use them to improve matching and to show tattoo shops anonymous, area-wide trends (for example, "40% of people near you want color work"), only for areas with enough quizzes that no single person can be picked out.
  • Searches and map area. When you search for a place or move the map, that search text and map area are sent to our map provider to show shops. We don't use GPS location.
  • Purchases. If a shop buys Premium or an event boost, the purchase is handled by Apple or Google. We receive the purchase status (for example, active until a date), never your card or bank details.
  • Basic technical data our service providers log to keep the service running and secure, such as IP address, device type and error logs. We use per-account limits (for example, how many quizzes per day) to prevent abuse.

Information stored only on your device

  • "I'm Booked!" reminders. The shop you booked and the review reminder are kept on your phone.
  • Face ID, Touch ID and fingerprint sign-in. Handled entirely by your phone. We never receive your fingerprint or face data; we only store your sign-in session in your phone's secure storage (Keychain or Keystore).

How we use information

  • To run your account and the app's features — matching, the map, favorites, events, reminders and shop accounts.
  • To verify that a person claiming a shop really runs it.
  • To process Premium subscriptions and boosts.
  • To improve matching and to give shops anonymous, area-wide quiz trends.
  • To keep the service secure, prevent abuse, and answer your messages.

We do not sell or rent personal information, share it for targeted advertising, or use it to build advertising profiles.

Service providers we use

We share information with these companies only as needed to run InkPass:

  • Supabase — accounts, database, file storage and server functions.
  • Mapbox — maps, place search and shop listings (receives search text and map areas).
  • Google — Sign in with Google, and Google ratings shown on quiz results (we send the shop's name and location, not information about you).
  • Apple and Google — app distribution, in-app purchases, and Face ID / fingerprint on your device.
  • RevenueCat — manages in-app purchase status for shop accounts.
  • Meta (Instagram) — we read public information from tattoo shops' Instagram business profiles to tag their styles and to check shop claim codes. No information about app users is sent to Meta.
  • Our email provider — sends sign-up and password reset emails.

We may also disclose information if required by law, to protect the rights and safety of our users or others, or as part of a sale or merger of our business (in which case this policy will continue to apply).

Information about tattoo shops

InkPass shows tattoo shops using business listings from our map provider. To match shops with quiz answers, our crawler (InkPassBot) reads shops' public websites and public Instagram business profiles and stores short tags about the shop — such as "fine line", "walk-ins" or "cover-ups" — not copies of their pages. Shop owners can claim their shop to correct their details, or contact us to have their tags removed.

How long we keep information

  • Account, profile, favorites and shop details: until you delete them or your account.
  • Anonymous quiz records: 13 months.
  • Abuse-prevention counters: 30 days.
  • Purchase records: as long as needed for accounting and legal requirements.

Your choices and rights

  • See and change your information in the app under Profile.
  • Delete your account in the app under Profile → Delete account, or through the contact form using the email address on your account. This deletes your account, profile, photo, favorites and any shop claim. Anonymous quiz records can't be traced back to you, so they aren't affected.
  • Notifications can be turned off in the app or in your phone's settings.
  • Subscriptions are managed and cancelled in your Apple or Google account settings.

Depending on where you live (for example, California and other U.S. states), you may have rights to know, access, correct, delete or get a copy of your personal information, and to not be discriminated against for using them. Send a request through our contact form (topic "Privacy request"); we'll respond within the time the law requires and may need to confirm it's you. We don't sell or "share" personal information as those laws define it.

Security

We use encrypted connections, access controls that limit each account to its own data, and secure on-device storage for sign-in sessions. No system is perfectly secure, so please use a strong password and keep your phone locked.

Children

InkPass is meant for adults 18 and older and is not directed to children. We don't knowingly collect information from anyone under 13. If you believe a child has given us information, contact us and we'll delete it.

Where information is processed

InkPass is available in the United States. Information is processed and stored in the United States by us and our service providers.

Changes to this policy

If we change this policy, we'll update the date at the top. For significant changes we'll also let you know in the app.
