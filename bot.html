InkPassBot

The web reader behind InkPass shop matching.

If you saw InkPassBot/0.1 (+https://inkpass.studio/bot) in your website logs, it was us. InkPass is an app that helps people find tattoo shops that fit their style. InkPassBot reads tattoo shop websites so the app can tell which shops do fine line, traditional, cover-ups, walk-ins and so on.

What it does

  • Visits only websites listed for tattoo shops on public business listings.
  • Reads up to 6 public pages per shop, about once a month.
  • Makes no more than 1 request per second to a site, and skips pages over 1 MB.
  • Follows robots.txt.
  • Doesn't fill in forms, log in or read anything behind a login.

What it keeps

Only short tags about the shop, such as "fine line", "walk-ins" or "custom designs", and the shop's website address. It does not store copies of your pages, photos or text, and never shows your content in the app.

How to block it

Add this to your site's robots.txt:

User-agent: InkPassBot
Disallow: /

InkPassBot also honors rules for its earlier name, InkMeBot, and rules for User-agent: *.

Run a tattoo shop?

Claim your shop in the InkPass app for free to set your own styles, walk-in policy and details, instead of relying on what we read. To have your shop's tags removed, or for any other question, use the contact form.
