Questions, problems or feedback — we read every message.

Contact us

Send us a message and we'll reply to your email, usually within 2 business days. Check the common questions below first — your answer may be there.

Common questions

I didn't get my sign-up or password reset email.

Check your spam or promotions folder, and make sure you typed your email correctly. Password reset emails contain a 6-digit code to enter in the app. Still nothing? Send us a message.

How does Match My Style pick shops?

We compare your answers with what each shop says about its own work — on its website, its Instagram and its InkPass card. Closer isn't always better: inside your travel distance, the best fit wins. Each card shows why it matched.

A shop's details are wrong.

If it's your shop, claim it in the app ("I'm a tattoo shop") and update the details yourself. If not, send us a message with the shop's name, city and what's wrong.

How do I claim my shop?

Create an account and choose "I'm a tattoo shop". Find your shop, then prove you run it by adding a short code to your Instagram bio or your website. It's free.

How do I cancel Premium?

Premium is billed through Apple or Google. On iPhone: Settings → your name → Subscriptions. On Android: Google Play → profile icon → Payments & subscriptions → Subscriptions. You keep Premium until the end of the period you've paid for.

How do I restore a purchase on a new phone?

Sign in to the same InkPass shop account, open Premium and tap "Restore purchases".

Delete your account

You can delete your InkPass account and its data at any time:

  • In the app: Profile → Delete account.
  • On this website: send us a message with the topic "Delete my account", using the email address on your InkPass account. We'll confirm by email and delete it within 30 days.

Deleting your account removes your profile, photo, favorites, event RSVPs and any shop claim and shop card details. Anonymous quiz records aren't linked to you and are removed automatically after 13 months. If you have Premium, cancel it in your Apple or Google account too — deleting your InkPass account doesn't stop app store billing.
